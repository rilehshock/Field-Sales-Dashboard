Welcome to the Field Survey Dashboard

A quick tour \u2014 reopen this anytime with the Help button in the toolbar.

What this is

  • Every KARMA survey our field team has logged on alcohol accounts this quarter \u2014 9,000+ visits \u2014 plus the buyer commitments tied to them.

    • The three tabs

  • Survey Overview \u2014 KPIs, the activity map, survey mix, top surveyors and chains.
  • Buyer Commitments Overview \u2014 open vs. fulfilled commitments by account, with a red-glow map of where open items sit.
  • Full Survey Detail \u2014 the complete searchable table with notes and photos, exportable to CSV.

    • Filtering

  • Use the toolbar dropdowns (Employee, Distributor, Chain, Channel, Premise). The green 📅️ date-range button opens a calendar: pick a preset (full period / last 30 / 14 / 7 days) or click any two dates for a custom range. The header shows the first and last survey dates in the data.
  • Click a state on the map to drill into just that state \u2014 click it again to clear.
  • Click a slice on any pie chart (Channel, Premise, Region) and the whole dashboard \u2014 map, KPIs, cards \u2014 refilters to that slice. Click it again to toggle off.
  • Reset clears every filter at once.

    • Cards & photos

  • Account cards show the rep's notes; click any thumbnail to open the full-size photo, or "Open original" for the KARMA viewer page.

    • Sharing

  • Snapshot downloads a shareable PNG of the current view with your active filters stamped on it.
  • The detail tab's Export CSV downloads exactly what's in view.

    • Feedback

  • Spot a wrong account, a bug, or want a new feature? Hit the green Feedback button (bottom-right) \u2014 your current filters are attached automatically.
  • Your login is remembered on this device if you tick "Remember me" at sign-in.

    • Report a glitch or request a change

    Your report goes to the site admin with your current tab and filters attached. Approved fixes ship in the next update.
